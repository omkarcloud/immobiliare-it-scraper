Prices per square meter of the zones in Milan

During October 2026, the asking price for properties for sale in Milan was highest in the Centro area, with € 11,094 per square meter. The lowest price, instead, was in the Bisceglie, Baggio, Olmi area, with an average of € 3,306 per square meter.
During the same month, the asking price for rental properties in Milan was highest in the Centro area, with € 31.11 per month per square meter. The lowest price, instead, was in the Bisceglie, Baggio, Olmi area, with an average of € 15.88 per month per square meter.
