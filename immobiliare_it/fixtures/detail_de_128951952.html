In the heart of one of Milan’s most sought-after and strategically located areas, we are pleased to offer for sale a welcoming, bright apartment on the second floor of an elegant building with a lift, concierge service and a well-maintained communal garden—features that make this a particularly peaceful and well-kept residential setting.

The apartment stands out for its practical layout and the pleasant natural light provided by its dual aspect. The entrance opens onto a spacious hallway that connects all the rooms harmoniously.

The spacious, functional kitchen has direct access to the first balcony, ideal for creating a small green corner or enjoying a relaxing moment outdoors. The private, welcoming living room benefits from excellent natural light and access to the second balcony, adding a greater sense of space to the living area.

The sleeping area comprises two well-proportioned bedrooms, suitable for a family or for anyone wishing to create a study or hobby room. The apartment is completed by a bathroom with a window and a practical mezzanine, perfect for additional storage or as a versatile space.

The property also includes a cellar, included in the sale price, with the option to purchase a garage separately.

The apartment is located at Via Avezzana 14, in a strategic, well-connected part of Milan, valued for its proximity to all essential amenities: supermarkets, schools, pharmacies, green spaces, shops and public transport, all within easy reach. An ideal home for those seeking a peaceful residential setting without giving up the convenience of city living.

Perfect for families, young couples or as a property investment in one of Milan’s most interesting and dynamic areas.
